Transform your Home Assistant dashboard into a masterpiece of Art Nouveau with this theme inspired by the legendary Czech artist **Alphonse Mucha** (1860-1939).

## 🎨 About the Artist

**Alphonse Mucha** was a Czech Art Nouveau painter and decorative artist who achieved fame in Paris with his iconic posters for Sarah Bernhardt [citation:4]. His style, known as "Le Style Mucha," is characterized by:

- **Pale pastel colors** - A departure from the bright posters of his contemporaries
- **Beautiful women in flowing robes** - Often with floral halos
- **Decorative scrollwork** - Inspired by nature and Byzantine art
- **Symbolic compositions** - Blending beauty with spiritual messages

> *"I was happy to be involved in an art that was for the people and not for private drawing rooms."* - Alphonse Mucha
